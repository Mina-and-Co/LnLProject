About Me

Hi! I'm Molly G. I was an eighth grade student at the Marylhurst School. The website you're using was created for my Leadership and Legacy project from scratch by me, with help from my awesome mentor! I used HTML, CSS, Javascript, node.js (with express), and sqLITE3 to make it. If you've ever wanted to try web development like I did, just know that it's REALLY hard! But I think it's super worth it. My project was a little harder than I thought it would be, so I might not recommend doing exactly what I did...but something like that would be fun! My advice to all soon-to-be-programmers is to not be afraid to look stuff up, but also don't be afraid to ask an actual person (or rubber duck) for help! Always remember to ask your mentor for help and listen to them no matter what your LnL project is.

Anyway, please use my website and use it correctly. STAY committed to whatever project you're working on right now, even if it's super annoying. Happy Coding!
